Fuel Price Saver

Helping truck drivers find the best diesel price on their route

What we do

Fuel Price Saver is a web app built for over-the-road truck drivers. It shows diesel prices at major truck stop chains along a driver's route and helps plan the best places to fuel.

Who we are

Fuel Price Saver is a product of RD Richards LLC, owned and operated since 2011. Our founder has driven truck since 1981 and has spent decades shopping fuel and building tools to analyze fuel prices.

Who we serve

Fuel Price Saver currently serves a small group of independent truck drivers, with plans to grow. Access is by membership only.
